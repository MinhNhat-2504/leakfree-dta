Software and data archive accompanying the manuscript "How much of our progress is leakage? A leakage-audited benchmark for drug-target affinity prediction".

Benchmarks that the drug-target affinity literature has reported on for a decade contain information that crosses the train/test boundary. This deposit measures how much, through which channel, and what is left of reported performance once the channels are closed.

WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

The label noise floor is 0.617 log units, measured as the pooled within-pair standard deviation across replicate measurements; 23.5% of replicate pairs disagree by more than tenfold. No model can be meaningfully scored below it.

Lookup leakage, the fraction of test rows whose label is recoverable because the same ligand-target pair appears in training, falls along the split ladder from 14.87% on a random split to 8.41% on a scaffold split, 1.42% on a ligand-cluster split, and 0.00% on the leakage-free split.

Closing one channel does not close the others. The protein_identity rung eliminates pair lookup completely while still leaving 78.87% of test rows with a protein at least 40% identical to one in training. It is named for the thing it fails to control.

On the leakage-free split, every re-run published model is beaten by a constant: GraphDTA 1.324, DeepDTA 1.328 and PerceiverCPI 1.328 RMSE, against 1.241 for predicting the training mean.

The registered shift-robust hypothesis was not supported. It is reported as a negative result rather than dropped, which is the reason for registering it before running it.

WHAT IS IN THIS DEPOSIT

leakfree-dta-v1.0.0-source.zip - the source archive of the tagged release, identical to GitHub tag v1.0.0 at commit 62ef9bd.

chembl_3fam_splits.zip - the split index, carrying each split's specification, fold counts and per-fold content digests, together with the full per-row fold assignment for every rung. Applying a split needs only this; rebuilding it from source needs the repository and a ChEMBL release number.

chembl_3fam_audit.zip - leakage reports per split and per target family, in JSON with per-row detail and in Markdown as summaries.

chembl_3fam_predictions.zip - per-row predictions, metrics and run provenance for every model evaluated.

chembl_3fam_tables_figures.zip - the tables and figures the manuscript reproduces.

MANIFEST.md - a SHA-256 digest for every archive above, so a download can be checked against what was deposited and compared against a later rebuild.

REPRODUCING IT

Source code: https://github.com/MinhNhat-2504/leakfree-dta

The benchmark is reproducible from a ChEMBL release number and the committed extraction query. Applying a split manifest to a dataset whose digest does not match is refused rather than warned about, because the failure it prevents is numbers that look plausible and mean nothing.
